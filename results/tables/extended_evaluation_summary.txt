INTERPRETATION NOTE
Sample-level classification accuracy should not be used alone to characterize an early-warning system. Event detection, timely detection, lead time and false-warning behavior are reported separately.

Confidence intervals should be interpreted at the run level because samples within a vehicle run are temporally correlated.

No comparison with results from another paper should be interpreted as a direct numerical superiority claim unless the datasets, vehicle class, prediction target and evaluation protocol are comparable.
